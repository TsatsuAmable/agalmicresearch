## Summary

A personal scholarly-training architecture that uses abundant machine cognition as an adaptive supply of examiners, critics, counterarguments, retrieval prompts and transfer challenges. It aims to expand durable human scholarliness while keeping claim-sensitive epistemic authority separate from training performance.

The governing empirical criterion is delayed or novel unaided transfer after AI scaffolding is reduced or removed.

## Immediate minimum viable protocol

1. Select one real epistemic artefact.
2. State an unaided interpretation and confidence.
3. Run CLAIM: Clarify, Locate, Attack, Inspect, Make your own judgment.
4. Undergo a five-question viva ladder.
5. State the current authority boundary and any handoff requirement.
6. Schedule a delayed transfer task.
7. Update the non-scalar Scholarly Capacity Profile from demonstrated performance only.

## Promotion condition

Promote beyond intake only if repeated use produces evidence of improved delayed or novel unaided performance, better calibration or stronger defensibility without disproportionate cognitive overhead.

## Reversal condition

Narrow or retire if the practice mainly improves AI-assisted output, creates scaffold dependence, produces generalized distrust, or offers no advantage over ordinary research practice and established learning methods.
