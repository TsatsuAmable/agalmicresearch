# Agalmic Research Direction Review — 20 September 2026

## Decision

Agalmic Research should stop presenting itself primarily as a growing catalogue of conceptual research directions.

Its strongest current form is a **small experimental laboratory for converting cognitive abundance into durable capability under real constraints**.

That laboratory has two owned modes:

1. **Empirical research** — test concrete allocation, validation and observability problems under cognitive abundance with protocols that can return PASS, CONDITIONAL or ABSTAIN.
2. **Capability practice** — use abundant machine cognition against real human constraints and test whether understanding, judgment, skill or practical capacity survives reduced scaffolding.

Independent systems such as Nemosyne can be used as bounded testbeds without becoming Agalmic-owned projects.

The v0.3 canonical definition remains sound. The execution model changes: fewer simultaneous programmes, clearer project ownership, and public emphasis on results rather than conceptual surface area.

## What the review found

### 1. The public narrative and the live work have drifted apart

The website currently emphasizes personal capability practice and older conceptual directions.

The repository's most substantial recent work is instead the empirical **Attention Allocation Under Cognitive Abundance** programme. It has already produced a real negative feasibility result:

- Track A v0.1 reached `ABSTAIN_PRE_OUTCOME`;
- citation outcomes remained sealed;
- the successor v0.2 design is blocked on selection-neutral historical state rather than implementation.

That is stronger evidence of Agalmic Research's method than another manifesto paragraph. It should be visible.

### 2. The Active Frontier is stale

The public frontier still allocates three slots to:

- personal capability practice;
- software engineering / leadership capability;
- Nemosyne discovery / reconstruction.

This duplicates the first two, hides the empirical attention-allocation programme, and blurs the ownership boundary around Nemosyne.

The frontier should instead expose:

- **Attention Allocation Under Cognitive Abundance** — flagship empirical programme;
- **Personal Capability Practice** — one applied stream containing scholarliness, engineering and leadership;
- **Nemosyne / Moneta** — an independent related testbed, not an Agalmic-owned programme.

The WIP limit remains useful, but fewer than three active owned commitments is acceptable.

### 3. Several research projects are really one lineage

Expert-Attention Preservation, Attention Allocation Under Cognitive Abundance, and parts of the handoff/triage work ask variants of the same question:

> How should scarce evaluation be allocated when candidate supply is abundant and observability is imperfect?

The stronger Attention Allocation programme should absorb that empirical lineage. Earlier projects remain evidence and provenance, not parallel programmes competing for attention.

### 4. The Cognitive Kernel has reached the point where more scaffolding is the wrong next move

The Cognitive Kernel now has:

- a consequence protocol;
- prospective pilot manifest;
- contamination controls;
- sealed execution receipts;
- paired planning machinery.

The next useful evidence is consequence-bearing matched work. Further framework development before running the pilot would risk process becoming the product.

### 5. The site has a good correction posture but an incomplete results record

The site already says that negative results and narrowed claims matter.

It does not yet show the strongest examples:

- the v0.1 precision-driven ABSTAIN;
- the v0.2 source-feasibility stop;
- the expert-attention preservation finding that acceptance-efficient ranking can destroy disagreement preservation.

These should become first-class public results.

### 6. The broad research map is useful as a possibility map, not as an activity map

The ten research tracks remain useful intellectual territory.

They should not imply ten concurrent programmes.

The public site should explicitly distinguish:

- **current empirical programme**;
- **current practice**;
- **supporting methods and infrastructure**;
- **preserved research questions**.

## Operating direction

### A. One flagship empirical programme

Until displaced by evidence, **Attention Allocation Under Cognitive Abundance** is the flagship scientific programme because it:

- is falsifiable;
- uses inherited open evidence before recruiting new participants;
- has explicit leakage and observability controls;
- can produce negative results;
- has already demonstrated a functioning pre-outcome firewall.

Its success criterion is not publication. It is a trustworthy answer or a trustworthy reason to abstain.

### B. One owned capability practice

The Personal Capability Practice remains the inward laboratory.

Software engineering, engineering leadership and scholarliness are domains inside that practice, not separate frontier projects.

The Cognitive Kernel is an intervention under test, not the identity of the practice.

### C. Independent projects remain independent

Nemosyne can supply real representation and verification problems and can consume useful Agalmic methods.

Agalmic Research does not own its product roadmap.

The same principle applies generally: integration does not imply ownership.

### D. Infrastructure earns attention only through active work

Frontier Throughput, provenance machinery, research-completion agents, Memory Palace conventions and site architecture are supporting infrastructure.

They should receive substantial work only when an active experiment demonstrates that the machinery is the binding constraint.

### E. Results before vocabulary

The website should increasingly answer:

- What was tested?
- What changed our mind?
- What stopped?
- What remains uncertain?
- What capability was created?

before expanding the ontology of the programme.

## Public information architecture

The public site should make these distinctions obvious:

- **Home** — compact programme orientation and the latest consequential result.
- **Projects** — ownership and current state of actual workstreams.
- **Practice** — inward capability-building method.
- **Research** — flagship empirical programme plus preserved question map.
- **Results** — negative, null, narrowed and positive findings.
- **Frontier** — scarce commitment, not a catalogue.
- **Explore** — full-resolution corpus and historical conceptual branches.
- **Publications** — durable outward objects only.

## Immediate changes adopted

1. Add a public Projects page.
2. Promote Attention Allocation to the Active Frontier and semantic knowledge index.
3. Collapse software-engineering and leadership capability into Personal Capability Practice rather than a separate frontier slot.
4. Mark Nemosyne explicitly as an independent related testbed.
5. Publish the v0.1 ABSTAIN and v0.2 source-feasibility stop on Results.
6. Add the current flagship empirical programme to Research and Home.
7. Update the semantic index so Explore no longer displays September 7 conceptual directions as if they were the current Active Frontier.
8. Add current empirical records to the research corpus.
9. Keep the canonical v0.3 definition rather than creating another definition version merely to fix execution drift.

## Next review trigger

Revisit this direction when one of the following occurs:

- Attention Allocation obtains a neutral source and reaches a real outcome result;
- the Cognitive Kernel consequence pilot produces a material capability effect or a negative result;
- a different programme produces stronger external evidence than the current flagship;
- the WIP limit itself becomes demonstrably harmful;
- project ownership or organisational form materially changes.

Until then, the default is:

> **Run fewer things, make the gates real, publish the reversals, and let capability or evidence earn the next project.**
